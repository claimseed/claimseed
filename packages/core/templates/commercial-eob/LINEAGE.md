# Lineage — commercial-eob

These are original claimseed layouts for an explanation of benefits (EOB) from a fictional self-funded employer health plan, administered by one of two fictional insurers, Fernhollow Health Plans and Quillmont Insurance Company. Each insurer's name, marks, colours, wording and contact details are invented, and neither layout imitates any insurer's statement.

One layout uses portrait pages. Page 1 holds a summary box with the plan's terms and the year's deductible and out-of-pocket totals, and each claim then has a page of its own. A closing page gives the remark codes, the No Surprises Act notice when a line is protected, and the appeal rights.

The other layout uses landscape pages. One table lists every claim, with the summary and the year's totals in a side panel, and a closing page gives the plan's terms, the remarks, the notice and the appeal rights.

Each structure follows the general shape of public consumer guides to reading an explanation of benefits, two per structure, which the project recorded when the layouts were written. No wording, figure or image was taken from them.

The content follows the rules for a notice of adverse benefit determination. Under 29 CFR 2560.503-1(g) (<https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-G/part-2560/section-2560.503-1>) each EOB gives the reason, the plan provision, the review procedures and their time limits, the right to bring a civil action under ERISA section 502(a), and the rule or rationale free on request; under 29 CFR 2590.715-2719(b)(2)(ii)(E) it also gives the claim's identity, the availability of the diagnosis and treatment codes, the denial code and its meaning, internal appeal and independent review by a reviewer outside the plan, and help contacts. DOL claims procedure FAQ C-12 treats an EOB that pays less than billed as such a notice.

Cost sharing is figured on the allowed amount, with no balance billing in network, as the Uniform Glossary describes (<https://www.cms.gov/files/document/uniform-glossary-english-060723.pdf>), and preventive care costs nothing in network under 45 CFR 147.130. The No Surprises Act protections and the in-network cost sharing on a protected line follow 45 CFR 149.110, 149.120, 149.410 and 149.420. The member disclosure, printed only on an EOB that has a protected line, follows PHS Act 2799A-5(c).

The only real contacts printed are two federal lines: the Department of Labor's Employee Benefits Security Administration and the federal No Surprises Help Desk. Every other phone number is in the fictional 555-01xx block.

No language tagline is printed, because the fictional members live only in counties not on the November 2023 CLAS county list (29 CFR 2590.715-2719(e)). Every page carries the claimseed specimen band and watermark, and every page with the insurer's header (all but the appeal page) also prints a SPECIMEN badge there.
