# Lineage — hmo-eob

Original claimseed portrait layout for a fictional Medicare Advantage HMO, "Tessara"; the name, wordmark, colors, wording and every contact detail are invented, and it imitates no carrier's statement.
Contents follow the monthly Part C EOB required by 42 CFR 422.111(k) (per-line billed, plan-approved, plan-paid and member amounts; year-to-date totals; out-of-pocket maximum progress; customer service, fraud reporting and appeal rights): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/422.111 and 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(2): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/422.2267
Section order follows the CMS Part C EOB model templates as described in CMS's implementation guidance, which includes an HMO example monthly EOB: https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/sites/default/files/hhs-guidance-documents/eob%20memo%206.17.13_221.pdf and https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/document/final-part-c-eob-model-templates-and-implementation-part-c-eob
Column vocabulary follows the CARIN Blue Button adjudication value set: https://hl7.org/fhir/us/carin-bb/STU2/ValueSet-C4BBAdjudication.json.html; the plain-language column explanations follow the CMS sample EOB: https://www.cms.gov/files/document/11819-sample-explanation-benefits-508.pdf
The nondiscrimination notice is the HHS Office for Civil Rights sample nondiscrimination policy for the 2024 Section 1557 rule, verbatim with the plan's name substituted, its optional bracketed parts omitted, and the grievance details written by claimseed: https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/1557-sample-nondiscrimination-policy.pdf. The English tagline is the small-size statement of the 45 CFR Part 92 Appendix B sample tagline, verbatim (https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2019-01-01/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-A/part-92/appendix-Appendix%20B%20to%20Part%2092); the other taglines are the language-assistance sentences of CMS's HealthCare.gov "Other Language Resources" page, verbatim, followed by a call line (https://www.healthcare.gov/language-resource/). data/SOURCES.md records every sentence's origin.
Every page carries the claimseed specimen band and watermark (stamped into the PDF by finalizePdf, independent of the HTML) and a SPECIMEN badge, and the cover says it is a synthetic test document not issued by any health plan.
