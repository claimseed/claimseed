# Lineage — ppo-eob

Original claimseed layout (portrait cover, landscape claim detail, portrait back pages) for a fictional Medicare Advantage PPO, "Corvane"; the name, wordmark, colors, wording and every contact detail are invented, and it imitates no carrier's statement.
Contents follow the monthly Part C EOB required by 42 CFR 422.111(k) (per-line billed, plan-approved, plan-paid and member amounts; year-to-date totals; deductible and out-of-pocket maximum progress; customer service, fraud reporting and appeal rights): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/422.111 and 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(2): https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/42/422.2267
Section order follows the CMS Part C EOB model templates as described in CMS's implementation guidance: https://www.hhs.gov/guidance/document/final-part-c-eob-model-templates-and-implementation-part-c-eob and https://www.cms.gov/medicare/health-drug-plans/managed-care-marketing/models-standard-documents-educational-materials
Column vocabulary (submitted, eligible, benefit, member liability, copay, coinsurance, deductible) follows the CARIN Blue Button adjudication value set: https://hl7.org/fhir/us/carin-bb/STU2/ValueSet-C4BBAdjudication.json.html; the plain-language column explanations follow the CMS sample EOB: https://www.cms.gov/files/document/11819-sample-explanation-benefits-508.pdf
The nondiscrimination notice is the sample notice of 45 CFR Part 92, Appendix A (2016 rule), verbatim with the plan's name and its fictional coordinator substituted: https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2019-01-01/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-A/part-92/appendix-Appendix%20A%20to%20Part%2092. The English tagline is the small-size statement of the 45 CFR Part 92 Appendix B sample tagline, verbatim (https://www.ecfr.gov/on/2019-01-01/title-45/subtitle-A/subchapter-A/part-92/appendix-Appendix%20B%20to%20Part%2092); the other taglines are the language-assistance sentences of CMS's HealthCare.gov "Other Language Resources" page, verbatim, followed by a call line (https://www.healthcare.gov/language-resource/). data/SOURCES.md records every sentence's origin.
Every page carries the claimseed specimen band and watermark (stamped into the PDF by finalizePdf, independent of the HTML) and a SPECIMEN badge, and the cover says it is a synthetic test document not issued by any health plan.
